# Valentin Bolshakov

**Computer Vision / Edge AI Engineer**

I work on real-time computer vision, embedded inference, and perception systems, with a focus on taking algorithms from prototyping to resource-constrained hardware.

My main areas of interest are **object detection and tracking, stereo vision, camera calibration, classical computer vision, and neural network deployment on edge devices**.

Most of my commercial and production work is private or NDA-protected. The repositories below contain independent tools, experiments, and engineering projects related to the areas I work in.

### Selected public projects

**[YOLO → RKNN Converter](https://github.com/jarvbol/yolo-rknn-converter)**
Toolkit for deploying YOLO-family detectors on Rockchip NPUs. Covers ONNX graph modification, INT8 post-training quantization, RKNN conversion, and reference inference.

**[Stereo Camera Calibration](https://github.com/jarvbol/stereo_calib_py)**
Configurable OpenCV pipeline for intrinsic and stereo calibration, reprojection-error analysis, outlier filtering, and estimation of relative camera pose.

**[BrightSpotDetector](https://github.com/jarvbol/BrightSpotDetector)**
Lightweight C++ / OpenCV detector for bright targets in grayscale, thermal, and IR imagery, with configurable ROI and detection parameters.

**[Electronics Workshop](https://github.com/jarvbol/ElectronicsWorkshop)**
Personal hardware projects covering the complete prototyping cycle from circuit design and firmware to assembly and enclosure.
